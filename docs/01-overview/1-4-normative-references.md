# 1.4 Normative References

The following Standard contains provisions that, through reference in this text, constitute provisions of this Standard. At the time of publication, the editions indicated were valid. All standards are subject to revision, and parties to agreements based on this Standard are encouraged to investigate and apply the most recent editions of the Standards published by these referenced sources.
