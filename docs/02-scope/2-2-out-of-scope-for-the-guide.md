# 2.2 Out of Scope for the Guide

- Details on implementation of [The Direct Standard®](https://directtrust.org/standards/the-direct-standard/versions).

- Details on how the Initiating System identifies the Direct address of the recipient.

- Subscription approaches that could be utilized in the notification process.

- Specific expected workflow behavior of Recipient Systems.

- Detailed specifications for standards-based Message Content Payloads or encapsulated content types.

- Notifications requiring interactive responses from recipients, e.g. request notifications, although similar information exchange mechanisms could apply.

- Guidance for implementers on additional Message Content Payloads or encapsulated content types in support of recipient workflow/routing. For additional information on this topic, consult the [Context IG](https://directtrust.org/standards/the-direct-standard/context-versions).

- Definitions of filtering mechanisms for specific event types by any actor are out of scope.
