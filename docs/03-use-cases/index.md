# 3 Use Cases

Unsolicited Notifications under this IG will typically involve two or more Business Actors or Actor Groupings.

An Initiating System and its associated HISP are responsible for complying with this IG. As such, there are alternative mechanisms to comply. In all cases, conformant HISP to HISP transactions SHALL comply with the IG, but some responsibilities may be handled by either the Initiating System or the Sending HISP.

Likewise, Receiving HISPs are responsible for complying with this IG. Receiving HISPs may tailor edge integration to the needs of a given Recipient System. This IG makes no additional constraints on the Recipient System. See Appendix [5.1.1.1](/05-value-sets-and-examples/5-1-value-sets-used-in-this-implementation-guide#examples-utilizing-loinc-codes-in-xdm-metadata) for sample implementation workflows observed during adoption testing.
