# 5.2 Additional Optional Content Payloads

These attachments MAY be included as well, either as MIME attachments or within an XDM package:

1)  HL7 FHIR Bundle conformant to the [Da Vinci Unsolicited Notifications Implementation Guide.](http://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-alerts/2020Feb/index.html) Such attachments should be provided as encapsulated data types conformant to the [Context IG](https://directtrust.org/standards/the-direct-standard/context-versions).

2)  2015 Edition C-CDA Transition of Care Documents.

This IG may be updated at a future date to require HL7 FHIR bundles as a permanent replacement for HL7 V2 ADTs.
